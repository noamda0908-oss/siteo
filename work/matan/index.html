Why it sticks

Florida is hard on sliding doors.

Sand, salt and sun wear doors out faster here. The good news: most doors don’t need replacing. They need new rollers and a clean, straight track.

01 · Sand

Grit in the track.

Every slide grinds fine sand into the rollers until the door drags, screeches and finally jumps the track.

02 · Salt

Salt in the air.

Coastal air corrodes bearings and roller housings, so the door gets a little heavier every month.

03 · Sun

Heat and humidity.

UV and heat bake seals and weatherstripping. Air, water and bugs start finding their way in.
